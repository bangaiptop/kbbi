# KBBI - Kamus Besar Bahasa Indonesia (Markdown Format)

## Overview

This repository contains the **Kamus Besar Bahasa Indonesia (KBBI)** — the official comprehensive dictionary of the Indonesian language — converted into individual Markdown (`.md`) files for easy access and machine-readable processing.

Each `.md` file represents a dictionary entry (headword/lemma) and includes structured information such as:

- **Lema (Headword)**: The main word or phrase being defined.
- **Homonim (Homonyms)**: Separate entries for words that share the same spelling but have different meanings.
- **Kelas Kata / Label (Part of Speech)**: Grammatical classification (e.g., *kata kerja*, *kata benda*, *kata sifat*, etc.).
- **Arti (Definitions)**: One or more meanings of the word.
- **Contoh (Examples)**: Usage examples demonstrating the word in context.
- **Bentuk Tidak Baku (Non-standard Forms)**: Informal or colloquial variants.
- **Varian (Variants)**: Alternative spellings or forms.
- **Turunan (Derivatives)**: Related words derived from the headword.
- **Gabungan Kata (Compound Words)**: Multi-word expressions.
- **Peribahasa (Proverbs)**: Traditional sayings.
- **Idiom (Idioms)**: Idiomatic expressions.

## Purpose

This Markdown-formatted KBBI dataset is designed primarily for:

- **Training local Large Language Models (LLMs)** — providing structured, high-quality Indonesian lexical data.
- **Fine-tuning** language models for better Indonesian language understanding and generation.
- **NLP research** — morphological analysis, word sense disambiguation, and semantic studies.
- **Reference** — developers and researchers can easily parse and query the dictionary programmatically.

## Structure

KBBI/ ├── a.md ├── abadi.md ├── abah.md ├── ... ├── z.md └── README.md


Each file is named after the headword (lowercase) and contains the full dictionary entry in Markdown format.

## Usage Example

Below is an example of how KBBI data can support language model output. The following poem was generated by **Qwen3-27B (Q4 quantized)**, demonstrating nuanced use of the word **"merayu"**:

---

### *Bisikan yang Merayu*

> Di sudut senja yang membisu,  
> Kau datang dengan langkah perlahan,  
> Membawa janji yang tak tertulis,  
> Namun terasa hangat di dada.
>
> Kau merayu dengan senyum tipis,  
> Bujuk rayu yang lembut bagai angin,  
> Menyentuh hati yang lelah menunggu,  
> Mengusir bayang kesepian yang dingin.
>
> Terkadang bisikmu membuatku iba,  
> Air mata mengalir tanpa sadar,  
> Kau merayu rasa pilu yang terpendam,  
> Sampai hati ini kembali terharu.
>
> Antara bujuk yang manis,  
> Dan air mata yang tulus,  
> Kau merayu jiwaku,  
> Hingga aku lupa pada waktu.

---

#### Penjelasan Penggunaan Kata:

- **"Kau merayu dengan senyum tipis..."** — Menggunakan makna *rayu* (2), yaitu membujuk atau memberikan hiburan untuk menyenangkan hati.
- **"Kau merayu rasa pilu yang terpendam..."** — Menggunakan makna *rayu* (1), yaitu menimbulkan perasaan iba, pilu, atau terharu.

This demonstrates how access to KBBI definitions enables LLMs to correctly distinguish between multiple senses of a word and apply them contextually.
